# Site image sources

| Asset | Source and tool | Prompt or input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `terrain-sani-1440.webp`, `terrain-sani-2880.webp`, `terrain-sani-mobile-780.webp` | Real terrain at Sani Pass, on the Drakensberg escarpment, from AWS Open Data Terrain Tiles (Terrarium elevation format). This area's elevation sources are SRTM and GMTED2010, courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. `site/scripts/terrain/topo.mjs` cuts a window from zoom 13 and resamples it, then draws a hillshade lit from the north-west, valley mist in the lowest ground (derived from elevation), 40 m contours and spot heights. `site/scripts/terrain/compose.mjs` layers them, captures each size with Playwright and encodes WebP in the browser. | Latitude `-29.595`, longitude `29.31`, zoom `13`, two tiles each side of the centre, a 896-pixel window resampled to 2304 pixels, 40 m contours with index lines every 200 m, spot heights for summits that stand clear within about 1.2 km. Desktop is captured at 2× (2880 × 1800) and 1× (1440 × 900), mobile at 780 × 1688. No image-generation prompt was used. See `site/scripts/terrain/README.md`. |
| `/example/generated-page.png`, `/example/cover-page.png`, `/example/next-page.png` | Captured from the repository's Switchback renderer by `site/example/capture.mjs` and Playwright at 2×. | `site/example/workbook.json` pages `W1-P2` and `W1-P1`, and `site/example/next-page.json` page `W2-P1`. All three are unmarked. The site lays the authored handwriting over `generated-page.png` as HTML text. |
| `site/src/assets/switchback-mark.svg`, `/favicon.svg` | Existing project-authored vector marks. | No generation prompt. The wordmark lockup keeps the existing mark beside letter-spaced SWITCHBACK. |

No shipped Switchback page or background was generated by an image model. Showcase pages are served from the renderer's own HTML.

The handwriting face is Kalam by the Indian Type Foundry, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`/fonts/kalam-LICENSE.txt`).
