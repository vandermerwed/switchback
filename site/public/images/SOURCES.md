This area's elevation sources are SRTM and GMTED2010, courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. `site/scripts/terrain/topo.mjs` generated a 25 m contour field and hillshade; Playwright captured the compositions and encoded WebP. | Latitude `-29.595`, longitude `29.31`, Web Mercator zoom `13`, two tiles each side of the centre, 25 m contours with index lines every 125 m.
