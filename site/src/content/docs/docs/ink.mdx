---
title: The ink language
description: The colour language — roles, structural marks, confidence marks, and the one-pen fallback.
---

The printer prints black on white; every meaningful mark after that is your own ink. Colour is
never the only carrier of meaning here — every role also has a letter, so the language still
works in black and white, on a photocopy, or for anyone who cannot distinguish the colours.

Run `switchback legend` any time to print your own mapping. What follows is the mapping itself.

## Roles

Roles are assigned to the pens you actually have, in priority order. A role with no matching pen
prints as a circled letter instead, so a black pen and a highlighter is already a complete kit.

| Role | Letter | Meaning | Suggested pen | On a proof, the agent… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ask | Q | a numbered question for the AI | blue | answers it |
| Stop | R | blocker, risk, broken, or not understood | red | challenges it |
| Keep | G | evidence, confident, something that worked | green | goes deeper |
| Crux | X | the one thing that matters most | highlighter | re-centres around it |
| Maybe | O | an idea, a possibility, a what-if | orange | explores it |
| Sense | P | gut feeling, intuition, felt reaction | purple | justifies it |
| Draft | D | provisional; do not trust yet | grey, or pencil | applies the edit |
| Reason | B | what you think; the main content | black | — |

Ask always claims blue whenever a blue pen exists, ahead of every other role. The rest are matched
by colour in the priority order above; you can also pin a specific pen to a specific role in
[desk mode](/docs/modes/desk/) instead of accepting the default.

## Structural marks

These carry meaning in any ink, on any page:

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ○ round an item | commit to it — on a proof, this locks the paragraph |
| ✕ through an item | rejected, but kept visible in the read-back |
| → | leads to, or causes |
| ▭ | this is a decision point |
| ? in the margin | revisit |
| ! in the margin | important |
| ✓ a tick | read, nothing to add |

## Confidence marks

Drawn beside a claim, in any ink:

| Mark | Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ● + | high | "I'd bet on this" |
| ◐ ~ | medium | "probably, but soft" |
| ○ ! | low | "guessing, unsure" |

Numbers written instead of dots ("50%", "80%") are read exactly as written and reported as such —
they are never converted into dots on your behalf.

## The one-pen fallback

Every role that has no matching pen becomes its circled letter, drawn in whatever ink you have.
That means the whole language still works with a single black pen: circle a Q instead of writing
in blue, a circled R instead of red, and so on. Nothing about read mode changes based on which
path you used to mark the page.
