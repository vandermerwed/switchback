# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-29)

The first public version.

- `/switchback`: one agent skill with four modes. **Workbook** makes pages to think on,
  **proof** prints a document to mark up, **read** reads the photos back, and **desk** saves the
  pens and paper you have.
- The `@vandermerwed/switchback` CLI: 37 components and 11 presets with graded evidence; five
  styles (Sitting, Series, Incubation, Ritual, Proof); the renderer and PDF output; `proof` for
  documents; `media` for phone photos (HEIC, JPEG and PNG); and `legend` for your pen mapping.
- A Claude Code plugin marketplace, and `npx skills add` for other agents.
